Featured Project

Bip Bip Driving School — Bilingual Driver Education Platform bipbipdrivingschool.com
Next.js · React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · Supabase (PostgreSQL)

A licensed North Texas driving school had no way to present its courses online, and its customers are split between English and Spanish speakers. I designed and built the production site that is now the school's storefront and enrollment funnel — sole developer, from client requirements through deployment.

  • Built a fully bilingual (Spanish / English) course site from a single codebase: every page, form label, and legal notice comes from a shared content layer, so the school updates copy once and both languages stay in sync instead of drifting apart.
  • Designed the site to satisfy Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) requirements for driver education providers — school license details, approved course and curriculum information, instructor credentials, and required student disclosures, presented clearly in both languages.
  • Modeled the course catalog, schedules, pricing, and translated content in Supabase (PostgreSQL) so non-technical staff can change offerings without a developer or a redeploy.
  • Took a mobile-first approach after seeing that nearly all traffic arrives on phones: responsive layouts, tap-friendly targets, and accessible contrast from a 360 px screen up through desktop.
